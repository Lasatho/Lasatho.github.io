## UAV Intrusion Detection (Master Thesis)

In my master’s thesis I am evaluating world models (JEPA, RSSM) for their applicability to embedded intrusion detection. For that I set up a simulation stack, combining several software tools with a custom interface, in order to generate training and evaluation data. I also configured an environment, consisting of a simulation PC, training PC, embedded device (Jetson Nano) and development pc and connected them via a VPN and built custom pipelines to automate the data flow.

## Nymgate (work in progress)

Nymgate is a proxy service, which eenables OT security architects to securely stream their data into the internet under strict security requirements. To do so, it routes traffic through the Nym network, a multi-hop mixnet with sphinx packet encryption to ensure the security of operational data, including metadata. The project is currently a proof-of-concept prototype. In the future I plan to modularize the code structure to make it adaptable to industrial communication protocols such as MQTT and OPC UA and add a secure management interface.
